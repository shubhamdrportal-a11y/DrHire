Plain-language summary: We collect the information you provide to match doctors with hospitals. We never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, apply for jobs, contact us, or use our services.

  • Personal Identification: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth
  • Professional Details: Medical degree, specialization, years of experience, MCI/NMC registration number, previous employers
  • Account Credentials: Email and encrypted password (via Supabase Auth)
  • Contact Enquiries: Messages submitted through our contact form
  • Job Applications: Details provided when applying for a listed position
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, IP address, and device information collected automatically

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account on the platform
  • To match doctor profiles with relevant job opportunities
  • To connect hospitals with qualified, verified candidates
  • To send email notifications about application status and job alerts
  • To respond to support tickets and enquiries within 24 hours
  • To improve platform features, performance, and user experience
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements in India
  • To prevent fraudulent activity and ensure platform integrity

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With Hospitals (with your consent): When you apply for a job, your profile is shared with the hiring hospital only
  • Service Providers: Supabase (database & auth), email delivery providers — bound by strict data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority in India
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, user data may be transferred (users will be notified)
We never share your contact details with hospitals without your explicit consent through the job application process.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with the following security measures:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies — you can only access your own data
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no system is 100% immune to breaches. We will notify affected users within 72 hours of any confirmed data breach.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies essential for the platform to function:

  • Authentication Cookies: To keep you logged into your account session
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-friendly analytics (no personal data collected)

You can disable cookies via your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent login functionality.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data at any time via your profile settings
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format (CSV/JSON)
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@doctorscoat.in with the subject "Data Rights Request".

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Active accounts: Data retained for as long as your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: All personal data removed within 30 days of deletion request
  • Job applications: Retained for 2 years for audit and compliance purposes
  • Contact form submissions: Retained for 12 months
  • Legal obligations: Some data may be retained longer if required by Indian law

8. Children's Privacy

Doctors Coat is a professional medical recruitment platform intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact us immediately and we will remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an email notification to all registered users
  • Display a prominent notice on the platform for 30 days

Continued use of Doctors Coat after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
