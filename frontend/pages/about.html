About Doctors Coat

Connecting Healthcare, One Placement at a Time

India's trusted platform for verified, fast, and free doctor recruitment.

Our Story

Built to Fix a Broken Hiring Process

Doctors Coat was started with a simple observation: hospitals were struggling to find the right doctors quickly, while qualified doctors were spending months navigating unverified listings and unresponsive recruiters.

We set out to build a platform where every listing is verified, every profile is genuine, and the process moves at the speed healthcare actually demands — free for doctors, transparent for hospitals.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To connect every qualified doctor in India with the right hospital opportunity — quickly, transparently, and at no cost to the doctor.

Our Vision

A healthcare system where no hospital bed goes unstaffed and no doctor's career stalls for lack of the right connection.

Our Promise

Verification first, speed second, and complete transparency with everyone we work with — doctor or hospital.

Our Values
