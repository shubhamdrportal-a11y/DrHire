FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

For Doctors
How do I register as a doctor?
Click "Login" in the nav → Register. Fill in your name, email and password. After email verification, complete your profile with qualifications and specialization.
Is registration free for doctors?
Yes — registration and job applications are 100% free for all doctors. We never charge candidates on our platform.
How long does it take to get a response after applying?
Hospital HR teams typically review applications within 2–5 business days. Our team follows up to ensure timely communication and you'll receive email notifications.
Can I apply to multiple jobs simultaneously?
Absolutely — there is no limit on applications. We encourage applying to all relevant positions matching your qualifications and location preference.
For Hospitals
How do I post a job vacancy?
Contact our team via email or phone with your vacancy details. We list the job within 24 hours. Hospital self-posting is coming soon.
How are doctor candidates verified?
All doctors submit medical registration certificates, degree certificates and valid ID. Our team cross-checks with MCI/NMC records before activating profiles.
What is the hiring turnaround time?
Standard positions: shortlisted candidates within 5–7 business days. Emergency hiring: pre-verified candidates within 24–48 hours via our express service.
Is there a placement guarantee?
Yes — we offer a 90-day replacement guarantee. If a placed doctor leaves within 90 days due to a fit mismatch, we find a replacement at no additional charge.
Account & Privacy
How do I reset my password?
Click "Login" → "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email and you'll receive a reset link within 2 minutes. Check your spam folder if needed.
Is my data safe on Doctors Coat?
Yes — data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security. Your information is never sold. You can request deletion by emailing hello@doctorscoat.in.
How do I delete my account?
Email hello@doctorscoat.in with subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email. We'll process it within 7 business days.
